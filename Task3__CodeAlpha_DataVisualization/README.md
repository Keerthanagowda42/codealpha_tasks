\# CodeAlpha Data Visualization



\## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data and creates meaningful visualizations using Python.



\## Objectives

\- Analyze sales and profit trends

\- Compare sales across categories and regions

\- Identify top-performing products

\- Analyze payment methods

\- Understand the relationship between sales and profit

\- Present insights through clear visualizations



\## Technologies Used

\- Python

\- Pandas

\- Matplotlib



\## Visualizations

1\. Monthly Sales Trend

2\. Sales by Category

3\. Sales by Region

4\. Top 10 Products

5\. Payment Method Distribution

6\. Sales vs Profit



\## Key Insights

\- Monthly sales show changes in business performance over time.

\- Different product categories contribute differently to total sales.

\- Regional sales help identify stronger-performing regions.

\- Top products contribute significantly to overall revenue.

\- Payment method analysis shows customer payment preferences.

\- Sales and profit are compared to understand business performance.



\## Dataset

The project uses the cleaned retail sales dataset generated during CodeAlpha Task 2 (EDA).
